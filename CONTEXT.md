# Domain

A selection is a source the operator liked or bookmarked on X. Every selection
is in scope regardless of topic or format. Selection is not an endorsement of
the source's factual claims.

A collection run retrieves the current account timeline from X into new private
storage. Coverage records pagination, termination, errors, and inaccessible items;
an empty response alone is not proof of complete historical coverage.

A capture is immutable retrieved source material with its location, retrieval
time, content digest, and authentic transport evidence. Research relates captured
material to claims. Claims distinguish factual support, attribution, opinion,
prediction, humor, and uncertainty. A reviewer independently evaluates the exact
proposal and its evidence. Publication commits verified knowledge and a readback
receipt. A source stays unresolved until its complete treatment passes.

A wiki contains source notes plus connected concepts/entities and useful indexes.
It must support retrieving knowledge and tracing it to evidence. A pile of status
files or summaries without verified source coverage is not a completed wiki.

The ledger owns durable workflow state; chat memory and worker completion do not.
New arrivals and backlog share the same quality contract. Credentials and all
personal corpus data stay private and separate from the public engine repository.
